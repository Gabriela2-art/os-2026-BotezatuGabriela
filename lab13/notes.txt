Lab 13 - Observe Answers

1. Without the lock, the letters mix because each line requires 31 separate write() system calls: 30 for the letters and one for the newline. The two processes can execute these calls concurrently, causing their output to interleave.

2. The two lines that make the whole line a critical section are:
   if (uselock) sem_down(3);
   if (uselock) sem_up(3);
   They ensure that only one process prints a complete line at a time.

3. A process can wake up and still find the semaphore value equal to 0 because wakeup() wakes all waiting processes, but only one can acquire an available unit. Another process may consume it first. Therefore, while is necessary to check the condition again.

4. If a process slept while holding the spinlock, another process could not acquire that lock to execute sem_up(). The waiting process would never be awakened, causing a deadlock. The spinlock must be released before sleep().
